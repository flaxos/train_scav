# Worldgen Corpus Protocol

## Goal
Use real Central European railway evidence to inform an abstract game grammar without shipping copied station geometry or a transformed map database.

## Corpus Target
Sprint 9 research focuses on small-town or rural secondary-line locations from Central Europe, with emphasis on:
- single-track corridors;
- passing loops;
- compact goods yards or loading tracks;
- agricultural/light-industrial sidings;
- town-road-station relationships;
- creek, bridge, culvert, cutting or embankment constraints;
- active, disused and abandoned freight-era infrastructure.

## Source Hierarchy
Prefer sources in this order:
1. Infrastructure-manager documentation and maps.
2. OSM/OpenRailwayMap topology and tags for candidate discovery and semantic hints.
3. Station plans or engineering drawings where access and licensing allow.
4. Historical maps for freight-era and settlement relationships.
5. Geolocated photographs for visual confirmation.
6. Enthusiast material only as a lead, never sole evidence for a rule.

## Classification Record
Each researched location should eventually record:
- stable location ID, country, name and coordinates;
- source references and retrieval dates;
- track count, passing-loop presence, goods/loading tracks, spurs, buffers and switch notes;
- settlement, road, waterway, bridge and industry relationships;
- freight legacy as active/disused/abandoned;
- game translation notes for shunting complexity, expedition density and salvage opportunities;
- confidence and governance labels.

## Licensing Rule
Do not copy real station layouts into shipped fixtures. Use source material to derive abstract relationships, probabilities and constraints. Keep OSM/ORM attribution and provenance in research docs/source registers, not in runtime semantic roles.
